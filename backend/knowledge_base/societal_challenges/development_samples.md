# Mock Societal Challenge Knowledge

These are development sample records for IMPACTX RAG testing.

Agriculture challenge: Small farmers often lack rapid access to crop disease diagnosis. Previous solutions used computer vision on leaf images, offline mobile capture, local-language advisory messages, and district agriculture officer review. Common technologies include convolutional neural networks, image quality validation, farmer mobile workflows, and cloud dashboards.

Healthcare challenge: Rural appointment and referral systems need offline-first registration, telemedicine queues, referral tracking, and district hospital escalation. Useful expertise includes public health, UX research, secure data handling, and health workflow integration.

Water and sanitation challenge: Rural pipeline leakage detection benefits from low-cost pressure sensors, acoustic sensing, flow analytics, and ward-level maintenance alerts. Civil engineering and IoT teams are usually required.

Disaster management challenge: Flood-prone villages benefit from river-level sensors, rainfall telemetry, siren alerts, SMS notifications, and local evacuation maps. Embedded systems, IoT networking, and disaster response planning are relevant.

Environment challenge: Smart waste segregation monitoring can use computer vision, bin sensors, route optimization, and citizen reporting.

Accessibility challenge: Navigation support for visually impaired citizens should include tactile markers, audio prompts, Bluetooth beacons, and inclusive field testing.
