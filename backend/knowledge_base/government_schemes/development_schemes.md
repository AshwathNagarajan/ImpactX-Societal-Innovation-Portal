# Mock Government Scheme Knowledge

These are development sample notes only.

Agriculture innovation projects should align with district agriculture extension workflows, farmer producer organization outreach, and state crop advisory mechanisms.

Water and sanitation projects should align with local panchayat maintenance ownership, Jal Jeevan Mission-style household service monitoring, and water quality reporting workflows.

Disaster management projects should align with district disaster management authority procedures, early warning communication chains, and village-level response planning.

Accessibility projects should follow inclusive public infrastructure principles, field validation with affected citizens, and local government implementation ownership.
